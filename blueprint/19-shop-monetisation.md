# 19 · Shop, monetisation and compliance

Owner of: every Robux product and its price, every cosmetic, passes and the subscription, catalysts, Supermutant variant sales, Mall Cash bundles, gifting, receipt handling, paid random item compliance, PolicyService rules, and the shop UI. If a price here and elsewhere differ, this file wins.

Decision (Mareux, 25 Oct): keep every monetisation item that doesn't break Roblox rules. Paid random items are built with full odds before purchase, live odds while a boost is active, a PolicyService fallback, an accurate maturity declaration, and Flea trading of paid items only where allowed.

## 19.2 Paid random items (compliance)

These are paid random items under Roblox's rules, directly (Robux) or indirectly (Mall Cash, which can be bought):

| Item | Where | Odds shown |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint Wheel spin (MC and Robux) | `12` §12.5 | full outcome table + each output model's chance |
| Full Reroll, Lock & Reroll (MC and Robux) | `13` §13.3 | chance of each value per attribute |
| Catalysts (probability modifier) | §19.7 | tier weights and blueprint chance before and after, per container class |
| Evac Pass Blueprint Crates | §19.6 | tier odds and models |

For every one:
1. Odds are shown **before** the purchase or MC spend, on the same panel, behind a labelled **Odds** button that opens a full table (`UI-SHOP-020`). Every table totals 100%. The tables are generated by `tools/check_numbers.py` from `Config` and checked against the code in F11 (`26`).
2. While a catalyst is active, its live odds are reachable from the HUD catalyst icon (`UI-HUD-070`).
3. `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync(player)` is called on join (retry 3×, 2 s apart). If it fails, treat the player as **restricted** (safe default). If `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted == true`:
   - Wheel spin buttons hidden; Fixed Trade-up offered (`12` §12.6).
   - Full Reroll and Lock & Reroll hidden; Rebalance offered.
   - Catalysts can't be bought, and the matchmaker doesn't put the player in catalyst raids (`20` §20.8).
   - Evac Pass crates become a fixed choice: "Pick any Rare blueprint" (`UI-PASS-010`).
4. `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed == false`: `PaidOrigin` items can't be listed or bought on the Flea (`16` §16.3), and Mall Cash bundles are hidden.
5. The experience's **Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire** declares: paid random items, violence (blood, realistic guns, fear), and free-form voice chat (voice is Roblox's own, eligible users only). Expected label: Moderate. Done in P1 (`26`).
6. All paid random item purchases are logged (`25` §25.8: `PaidRandomEvent` with odds version).

## 19.3 Purchase handling (`Services.Shop`)

- **Developer products** for anything repeatable or saved on the profile; **game passes** only for the Merchant Tax Pass. (Entitlements such as flats are developer products saved on the profile, so they survive Move-in and data moves.)
- `MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt`:
  1. Load the buyer's profile (if not in this server, return `NotProcessedYet`).
  2. If `receipt.PurchaseId` is in `profile.receipts` (last 500 kept), return `PurchaseGranted`.
  3. Grant per the product's handler (below). If the in-game conditions are no longer true, grant the product's **token** instead (Move Ticket, Mutate Token, Revive Token, Insurance Credit, Spin Credit, Press Credit), shown in mail.
  4. Add the PurchaseId, save the profile (`ProfileStore` save and wait), then return `PurchaseGranted`. If saving fails, return `NotProcessedYet`.
- Prompt state: only one prompt at a time per player; the UI disables the button until `PromptProductPurchaseFinished`.
- Every grant writes `EconomyEvent` (`25` §25.8).

### Pricing rules

- Prices below are the base prices. Turn on Roblox **regional pricing** for every developer product and pass in P1 (Creator Hub → Monetization), so players in lower-income regions see local-adjusted prices. Never show a hard-coded Robux number in UI; always read the product's live price from `MarketplaceService:GetProductInfo` (cached 10 min).
- **Price ladder:** cheapest useful buy 25 R$ (Full Reroll, Name Tag), then 49, 79, 99, 199, 299, 399, 499 to 1,499. No single product over 1,499 R$.
- **No first-session offers and no pop-up offers ever** (`28` §28.2). There's no limited-time starter bundle; the first purchase prompt a player sees is one they opened.
- Insurance has a Mall Cash price (`16` §16.4), the weekly Mall Condition is free for everyone (`28` §28.3), and a catalyst raid is shared by every player in it, not only the buyer. Second Wind and Mutate Now are Robux-only by Mareux's decision in the review. They're the products most likely to feel pay-to-win to survivors, so L1 (`28` §28.5) checks their purchase rate against survivor extraction in Lockdown raids.

## 19.6 Evac Pass (season pass)

- Seasons are 8 weeks, starting Mondays (`28`). 50 tiers. Pass XP = raid XP + contract XP. Tier n needs 1,000 Pass XP. Forgiving: weekly contracts give 3,000 Pass XP, and the last 2 weeks give double Pass XP (catch-up).
- **Free track** (every 2 tiers, 25 rewards): MC (2,000–5,000), stickers, Name Tag ×2, one finish, one emote, Blank Vinyl at tier 50.
- **Premium track** (every tier, 50 rewards, 499 R$): 4 finishes, 2 outfits, 2 charms, 1 death effect, 1 extraction flare, 3 Name Tags, 2 Blank Vinyls, 20,000 MC total, a season door banner, a **Haul Bonus** (+10% MC on Pawnbroker sales for the rest of the season), and **Blueprint Crates** at tiers 10, 20, 30, 40.
- **Blueprint Crate:** a random blueprint of a random category: Common 40%, Rare 35%, Epic 20%, Exotic 5%, model uniform within tier. Odds shown on the pass page before purchase (§19.2). Crate outputs are `PaidOrigin`. Restricted players get "Pick any Rare blueprint" instead.
- Unclaimed rewards are kept and auto-claimed at season end (mail).
- Pass page `UI-PASS-001`.

## 19.7 Catalysts (raid modifiers)

- Sold by **Sully** at the Black Market (`UI-CAT-001`) for 99 R$ each, one raid. The squad leader buys one before queueing; it applies to that squad's next raid. One catalyst per raid (if two squads both bring one, the matchmaker never puts them in the same raid).

| Catalyst | Map effect | Loot effect (shown as before/after tables) |
|---|---|---|
| **Blood Moon** | Red sky through skylights, zombie count ×1.5, Walkers +15% speed, `MUS-RAID-040` | Epic weight ×1.5, Exotic weight ×2; blueprint chance ×1.5 |
| **Blackout** | All generators broken (can't be fixed), emergency lights only, The Janitor enters at 360 s | Rare+ weights ×1.25; blueprint chance ×1.5 |
| **High-Roller Supply Drop** | At 420 s a supply crate parachutes onto one of 3 roof or atrium points, with red smoke and a mall-wide landing noise (120, heard everywhere) | Supply crate (`MDL-CON-020`, 8 slots, weights 0/0/40/45/15, blueprint chance 50%) |

- **Matchmaking:** a catalyst raid is filled from the same pool with players who have **Allow catalyst raids** on (default on, Settings) and aren't restricted (§19.2). Everyone in the raid sees the catalyst on the deploy screen with an Odds button, and the HUD icon (`UI-HUD-070`) during the raid.
- **Free Weekly Condition** (`28` §28.3) always applies too and stacks with a catalyst.
- Private raids can use catalysts (the leader buys).

## 19.9 Mall Cash bundles

- Five bundles (§19.4). Hidden when `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed == false`. A 24 h cap of 400,000 MC bought per account (anti real-money trading).
- Mall Cash bought with Robux isn't flagged; the Flea price limits (`16` §16.3) stop laundering.

## 19.10 Gifting

- From the shop, **Gift** on any Robux cosmetic or furniture item, then pick a Roblox friend who has played the game. The gift arrives in their mail with your display name. Not available for consumables, flats, titles, passes or bundles.
- Players who are restricted from receiving the item type (none at launch) don't appear in the list.

## 19.12 Mall VIP (subscription)

- Roblox experience subscription, monthly, at the lowest standard price tier (set in Creator Hub in P1). Cancel any time through Roblox (the VIP panel links to it).
- Benefits while active: **daily stipend** 1,500 MC (claim at the flat terminal, once per UTC day, no stacking), +1 Flea listing slot, VIP nameplate frame and gold door number plate, one exclusive outfit cosmetic per month (kept forever), workbench craft time −10%.
- No combat or loot advantage.
