# 13 · Survivors, permadeath, progression, contracts and achievements

Owner of: survivor generation and appearance, permadeath, the Survivor Draft, stat reroll and Rebalance, account level and XP, unlocks by level, reputations (Merchant Score, Mutant Rep, Vendor Loyalty), the Memorial Wall, the Collection Book, daily and weekly contracts, achievements and trophies, and tracked stats.

---

## 13.1 Survivors

- A **survivor** is the character you play. Each has: a generated name (first + last from `Survivors.Names`, 120 × 120 lists in `src/shared/Names.luau`, built from `data/names_first.txt` and `data/names_last.txt`), a face, hair, skin tone and build preset (12 faces, 16 hair styles, 8 skin tones, 3 builds; `MDL-CHR-001…039`), five attributes (`02` §2.4), and a raid count.
- Survivors use the game's own R15 character models, not Roblox avatars, to keep the grounded tone. The player's **outfit cosmetics** (`19` §19.5) are worn by every survivor they play.
- **Name display:** survivor names are shown only to squadmates (HUD squad list) and in your own flat. Strangers see no name tags in raids. In the tower, players show their Roblox display name above their head (`14` §14.8).

## 13.2 Permadeath and the Survivor Draft

- When a survivor dies (`03` §3.8), it's retired. Its attributes, any role title (`17`) and its raid count are gone. Account level, XP, Mall Cash, stash, flat, blueprints, cosmetics and reputations stay.
- **Survivor Draft** (`UI-SUR-001`): opens on your next visit to the flat (it blocks deploying until done). Shows **3 generated survivors** side by side (portrait, name, the five attributes as pips). Pick one, free.
- **Generation:** start every attribute at 3 (15 points), then add the remaining 10 points one at a time to random attributes that are below 7. Every survivor totals 25.
- New accounts draft their first survivor before the Orientation Run (after a 3 s intro cut of the tower, `03` §3.9).

## 13.3 Stat reroll (paid, kept) and Rebalance

| Option | What it does | Price | Random? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full Reroll | Re-generates all five attributes of your current survivor (§13.2 method) | 20,000 MC or 25 R$ | yes (paid random item) |
| Lock & Reroll | Lock one attribute at its current value, re-generate the other four (they share the remaining points, each 3–7) | 75 R$ (Robux only) | yes (paid random item) |
| Rebalance | Move 1 point from one attribute to another (both stay within 3–7) | 8,000 MC | no |

- **Odds** (generated by `tools/check_numbers.py`; shown behind the Odds button):

| Value | Full Reroll, any one attribute | Lock & Reroll, each other attribute, locked at 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 9.68% | 3.9% / 6.1% / 9.0% / 12.7% / 17.5% |
| 4 | 26.28% | 16.8% / 21.5% / 26.3% / 31.2% / 35.8% |
| 5 | 30.81% | 28.6% / 30.8% / 31.9% / 31.6% / 29.8% |
| 6 | 20.82% | 26.8% / 24.5% / 21.3% / 17.4% / 13.2% |
| 7 | 12.41% | 23.9% / 17.1% / 11.5% / 7.1% / 3.8% |

- Available only from the **Survivor Terminal** in your flat (`15` §15.3), and only before the survivor's first raid (raid count 0). This keeps it away from moments right after a death.
- **Compliance:** the generation method and the resulting probability of each value per attribute are shown before purchase with an **Odds** button (`STR.SUR.ODDS`, table generated by `tools/check_numbers.py` and pasted into `Strings`). `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` → Full Reroll and Lock & Reroll are hidden; Rebalance stays. MC rerolls count as indirect paid random items (MC is buyable).

## 13.4 Account level and XP

- XP sources: raid results (`03` §3.8), contracts (§13.8), achievements, first-time Collection Book entries (+20 each).
- **Level curve:** XP to go from level n to n+1 = 400 + 120 × (n − 1). Max level 100. Level 100 total = 621,720 XP (checked in `tools/check_numbers.py`).
- Level-ups: toast `UI-HUD-050`, `SFX-UI-040`, `STR.PRG.LEVELUP`, and a reward.

| Level | Unlock / reward |
|---|---|
| 2 | 2,000 MC |
| 3 | Daily contracts unlocked (before then you get the 3 starter contracts, §13.8) |
| 5 | **The Flea** (`16`) |
| 8 | Survivor Rebalance |
| 10 | Role titles for MC (`17`), Fixed Trade-up |
| 15 | Bounty Hunter title for MC |
| 20 | Executive Suite purchasable with MC (it's always buyable with Robux) |
| 25 | Blueprint Wheel MC spins |
| every 5 levels from 30 | an outfit cosmetic from the Level Track (`19` §19.5) |
| 50, 75, 100 | nameplate frames (tower), "Veteran" titles |

## 13.6 Mutant Track

- Cosmetics for your mutant forms, unlocked by Mutant Rep: 100 Stalker skin "Rotten", 250 Supermutant roar "Grave Howl", 500 Stalker skin "Ashen", 1,000 Supermutant skin "Chitin", 2,000 mutation VFX "Black Bloom", 5,000 Supermutant skin "Leviathan". Assets `MDL-MUT-020…025`, `SFX-MUT-040`, `VFX-MUT-020`.

## 13.7 Memorial Wall and Collection Book

- **Memorial Wall** (in every flat, `15` §15.3): a plaque for each retired survivor: name, raids survived, best haul, cause of death. Up to 50 shown. Turns permadeath into a story rather than a punishment.
- **Collection Book** (`UI-COL-001`, opened from the flat): every item id in the catalogue, greyed until first extracted. Sections by group (weapons, gear, medical, valuables, blueprints, Relics). Completing a section grants a sticker cosmetic and +500 XP. The book starts with 5 entries already filled from the Orientation Run (endowed progress).

## 13.8 Contracts

- **Starter contracts** (level 1–2): "Extract once", "Open 10 containers", "Win a struggle". Each: 500 MC + 100 XP.
- **Daily contracts:** 3 per day, given by the **Contract Board** in the atrium (`14` §14.3) and the flat terminal. Reset at 00:00 UTC. One free reroll per day. Each is doable in one raid. Reward 1,500 MC + 150 XP each; all three: +1,000 MC bonus. Unclaimed contracts stay claimable until the next reset (completing in raid auto-claims on return).
- **Weekly contract:** one, reset Monday 00:00 UTC (fresh-start landmark). Reward 8,000 MC + 800 XP + a sticker.
- **Contract pool** (`src/shared/Contracts.luau`): extract with X MC of loot, open N containers in a wing, kill N zombies with melee, take down N Walkers, start a generator, extract with a Rare+ blueprint, cure a Sleeper (yourself), revive a stranger, survive a Lockdown, use a scanner, extract from the Helipad, extract from the Garage, craft N items in raid, break N glass panes, extract without firing a shot, play as a Ghost and cause a struggle.
